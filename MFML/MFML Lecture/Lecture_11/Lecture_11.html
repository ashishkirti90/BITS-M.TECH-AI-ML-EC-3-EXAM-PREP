How to read this scene

  • The surface is the loss landscape. Height = how wrong the model is.
  • Every glowing ball solves the same problem with a different update rule.
  • The tube behind a ball is its history. The floor map shows the same paths from above (contour view).
  • One step = one parameter update. Getting low in fewer steps = winning.

Steps are multiplied by a fixed display scale so motion is visible. The Live Math tab shows the exact numbers used.

Standard Gradient Descent

A hiker in thick fog. Feel the slope under your feet. Step downhill. Repeat.

$$ w_{t+1} = w_t - \alpha \, \nabla f(w_t) $$

Weak spot: in a narrow valley the side walls are much steeper than the floor, so it bounces wall-to-wall (zig-zag) instead of walking along the valley.

Momentum

A heavy bowling ball. Its direction is an average of recent slopes, so sideways zig-zags cancel out and the forward part adds up.

$$ v_t = \beta v_{t-1} + (1-\beta)\nabla f_t, \qquad w_{t+1} = w_t - \alpha v_t $$

β close to 1 = heavier ball. It also rolls through small bumps and flat patches.

Nesterov (NAG)

Momentum, but it looks before it leaps: first peek where the momentum is carrying you, measure the slope there, then correct.

$$ v_t = \beta v_{t-1} + (1-\beta)\nabla f(w_t - \alpha\beta v_{t-1}) $$

Because it senses the slope ahead, it starts braking before it overshoots.

Adagrad

Every direction gets its own step size. Directions that have seen big gradients get small careful steps; quiet directions get bold ones.

$$ G_t = G_{t-1} + (\nabla f_t)^2, \qquad w_{t+1} = w_t - \frac{\alpha}{\sqrt{G_t}+\epsilon} \odot \nabla f_t $$

Weak spot: G only ever grows, so the steps shrink forever. Adagrad retires early, even far from the bottom.

RMSProp

Adagrad with forgetting. Recent slopes count; old ones fade away (a moving average). So the step size adapts to the terrain you are on now.

$$ E[g^2]_t = \beta E[g^2]_{t-1} + (1-\beta)(\nabla f_t)^2 $$

Adam

The greatest hits album: Momentum (first moment m) + RMSProp (second moment v) + a warm-up correction so the first steps are not too shy.

$$ \hat m_t = \frac{m_t}{1-\beta_1^t}, \quad \hat v_t = \frac{v_t}{1-\beta_2^t}, \quad w_{t+1} = w_t - \frac{\alpha}{\sqrt{\hat v_t}+\epsilon}\,\hat m_t $$

The default optimizer of modern deep learning.

Things to try

  • On the Ravine, raise α slowly until GD explodes. Find the edge of stability.
  • On the Saddle, double-click almost exactly on the ridge line. Who escapes first, and why?
  • On the Bumpy Bowl, use α = 0.05, β = 0.95. GD parks in a dip; Momentum rolls through it.
  • On Four Wells, double-click different start points — optimizers can land in different minima.
  • Let Adagrad run long anywhere and watch its steps shrink to nothing.
